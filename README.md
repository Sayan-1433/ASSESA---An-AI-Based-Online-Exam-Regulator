# ASSESA---An-AI-Based-Online-Exam-Regulator
Assesa is an AI-powered platform for real-time assessment monitoring, intelligent event detection, and efficient exam review.

Assesa is a proposed AI-powered assessment integrity platform designed to help educational institutions conduct online examinations more reliably, transparently, and efficiently. Its primary objective is to support the detection of potential irregularities during assessments while reducing the burden of continuous manual monitoring on human reviewers.

The platform focuses on targeted detection using computer vision and audio analysis to identify relevant events, such as the presence of additional people, unusual device usage, changes in candidate visibility, and poor video quality. Where appropriate, optional screen and browser monitoring can provide additional context, subject to candidate consent and institutional policies.

Assesa aims to transform raw monitoring data into structured, explainable event reports rather than relying solely on continuous video playback. Its proposed reviewer workspace would allow authorized personnel to examine flagged events, review supporting evidence, assess their significance, and make informed decisions. A centralized dashboard could also provide real-time monitoring indicators, assessment status, and analytics to help institutions understand patterns across examinations.

The platform is built around four proposed goals: **unified, accessible, real-time, and analytics-driven**. These principles aim to bring essential monitoring information together, simplify the review process, provide timely insights, and support evidence-based decision-making.

Assesa is currently a concept-stage proposal. Its effectiveness, accuracy, privacy safeguards, and practical feasibility would need to be evaluated through implementation and testing. Ultimately, it seeks to make online assessment monitoring more manageable, transparent, and accountable.
